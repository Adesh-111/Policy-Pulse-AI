# Northbridge College Student and Staff Data Privacy Policy

**Version:** 2.1 (Old)  
**Effective date:** 1 April 2023  
**Policy owner:** Information Technology Office

## 1. Principles

The college collects personal data for specified academic, employment, welfare, security, and legal purposes. Access is limited to personnel who need the information for an assigned duty. Data must be accurate, protected against unauthorized access, and not retained indefinitely.

## 2. Responsibility

The Information Technology Office is the central owner of information security and privacy controls. Departments follow IT instructions and report suspected incidents to the service desk. The Registrar approves requests to disclose student records outside the college.

## 3. Approved storage and sharing

Personal data must be held in college-managed systems. Email attachments containing sensitive data must be encrypted. Public links and personal cloud drives are prohibited. Vendors may process data only under a contract approved by the Registrar and IT Office.

## 4. Retention

Student records, including applications, attendance records, examination working records, advising notes, and service requests, are retained for **five years after the student leaves the college**. Permanent degree registers and final transcripts are retained indefinitely. Staff working records are retained for five years after employment ends.

At the end of the period, the department that created the record requests deletion from IT. A legal hold suspends deletion.

## 5. Incident response

A user must report a suspected loss, unauthorized disclosure, or compromised account as soon as practicable. The IT Office assesses the event and informs senior management within **72 hours** when material harm is possible.

## 6. Individual requests

The Registrar coordinates requests to access or correct personal data. Departments search their records and respond to the Registrar within ten working days.
