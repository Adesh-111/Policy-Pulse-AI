# Northbridge College Campus Placement Eligibility Policy

**Version:** 1.3 (Old)  
**Effective date:** 1 June 2024  
**Policy owner:** Career and Placement Office

## 1. Baseline eligibility

A final-year student may register for campus placement when the student has a cumulative grade point average of at least **6.5 on a 10-point scale**, has **no active backlog**, and has met the college attendance requirement of **75%**. A recruiting employer may publish a higher threshold.

## 2. Conduct and preparation

The student must complete the placement orientation, submit an accurate résumé, and attend any interview accepted through the portal. Falsified information or an unexplained absence may suspend access for the current recruitment cycle.

## 3. Offers

A student who accepts an offer may attend one later process only when the advertised salary is at least 50% higher. The Placement Officer records acceptance and releases the candidate list to employers.

## 4. Exceptions

The Principal may permit a student with one recently cleared backlog to register when the final result has not yet reached the student system. No exception is available for the 6.5 CGPA threshold.

## 5. Responsibility

The Career and Placement Office verifies CGPA, backlog, and attendance from reports supplied by the Examination Cell and academic departments. Students must report any discrepancy before applying to an employer.
