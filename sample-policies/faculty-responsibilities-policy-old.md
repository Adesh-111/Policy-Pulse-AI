# Northbridge College Faculty Academic Responsibilities Policy

**Version:** 2.5 (Old)  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2024  
**Policy owner:** Dean of Academics

## 1. Course preparation

Faculty members publish the syllabus, reading list, assessment pattern, office hours, and attendance method within **ten business days after classes begin**. Material changes require the Head of Department's approval and reasonable notice to students.

## 2. Teaching and attendance records

Faculty conduct scheduled classes, record attendance within two working days, and retain source corrections through the semester. Monthly totals are sent to the department office for shortage notices.

## 3. Assessment and feedback

Assessment instructions state the learning outcomes and grading criteria. Marked coursework with meaningful feedback should be returned within **15 business days**. Faculty keep a grade calculation record until one year after the result is final.

## 4. Artificial intelligence in assignments

A faculty member may authorize a named generative AI tool for a particular graded assignment when the learning benefit is explained in the brief. The faculty member defines attribution requirements and an alternative for a student who cannot access the tool. Otherwise, the college student AI prohibition applies.

## 5. Examination duties

Faculty complete paper setting, invigilation, marking, and moderation assigned by the Head of Department. Verified marks are sent directly to the Examination Cell within the examination policy deadline.

## 6. Student support and confidentiality

Faculty offer published consultation time and refer serious welfare concerns to Student Affairs. Student grades, disability information, and advising notes must not be disclosed to another student or stored in a personal cloud drive.
