# Northbridge College Campus Placement Eligibility and Fair Access Policy

**Version:** 2.0 (New)  
**Effective date:** 1 June 2026  
**Policy owner:** Career Development Centre

## 1. Baseline eligibility

A final-year student may register for campus placement when the student has a cumulative grade point average of at least **7.0 on a 10-point scale**, has no more than **one active backlog**, and has attendance of at least **75% in each current course**. An employer may set a higher CGPA or prohibit active backlogs when that condition is published before applications open.

## 2. Conduct and preparation

The student must complete orientation, consent to share the application profile with the selected employer, and certify résumé accuracy. An unexplained missed interview triggers review; it does not automatically suspend the student before an opportunity to respond.

## 3. Offers and fair access

After accepting an offer, a student may enter one later process when its advertised annual compensation is at least 30% higher or the role is in a separately declared public-service category. The portal records consent, applications, withdrawals, and offer decisions.

## 4. Exceptions

The Placement Eligibility Panel may allow a student below 7.0 but at or above 6.8 to apply to an employer that has expressly accepted the lower band. The Panel cannot waive an employer's published criteria. A disability-related adjustment may change the selection process but not an academic criterion imposed by an employer.

## 5. Responsibility and data source

The Career Development Centre retrieves CGPA and backlog status from the locked examination system. **Academic departments remain responsible for certifying the 75% course-attendance condition** before each recruitment window. Students may request correction through the source office; placement staff must not edit academic data.

## 6. Retention

Employer application profiles and consent records are retained for three years after graduation, after which the Centre requests verified deletion under the privacy policy.
