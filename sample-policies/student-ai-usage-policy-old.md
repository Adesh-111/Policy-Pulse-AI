# Northbridge College Student Use of Artificial Intelligence Policy

**Version:** 1.0 (Old)  
**Effective date:** 1 August 2024  
**Policy owner:** Academic Integrity Committee

## 1. General prohibition

Students must not use generative artificial intelligence to create, rewrite, summarize, translate, or substantially edit material submitted for a graded assignment. The prohibition applies even when a student discloses the tool or when a tool produces only part of the submission.

## 2. Limited exceptions

Ordinary spelling correction, screen readers, speech-to-text, and institution-provided grammar checking are permitted when they do not generate new ideas or analysis. A disability accommodation approved by Student Affairs may permit another assistive use.

## 3. Research and practice

Students may experiment with generative AI in an ungraded classroom exercise when the instructor supervises the activity. AI output must not be copied into a later graded submission. Students must not enter personal data, confidential college information, unpublished research, or assessment material into an external service.

## 4. Detection and review

An automated detector score is an indicator only and cannot establish misconduct by itself. The course teacher must identify specific evidence and give the student an opportunity to explain the work. Confirmed unauthorized use is handled under the Academic Integrity Code.

## 5. Responsibility

Course teachers include the prohibition in every assessment brief. Students retain drafts and sources until the grade is final. The Academic Integrity Committee reviews this policy annually.
