# Northbridge College Faculty Academic and Compliance Responsibilities Policy

**Version:** 3.0 (New)  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026  
**Policy owner:** Academic Affairs Division

## 1. Course preparation

Module leaders must publish the syllabus, accessible reading list, assessment pattern, office hours, attendance method, and all assessment AI-use labels within **five business days after classes begin**. The department curriculum coordinator verifies completeness in the learning system.

## 2. Teaching and attendance records

Faculty record attendance by the end of the next business day and resolve supported corrections within three business days. They verify the weekly attendance dashboard. Academic advisors must document contact with a student flagged below the warning threshold.

## 3. Assessment and feedback

Assessment instructions state outcomes, grading criteria, accessibility arrangements, and permitted resources. Marked coursework with actionable feedback must be returned within **10 business days** unless the Head records a cohort-wide extension before the deadline. Grade calculations are retained under the central records schedule.

## 4. Artificial intelligence in graded work

To protect independent authorship, faculty must label every graded assignment **“AI-generated assignment content prohibited.”** Faculty may demonstrate generative AI in an ungraded exercise, but they must not authorize generated or rewritten content in a graded submission. Suspected use must be reviewed with evidence; a detector score alone is insufficient.

## 5. Examination duties

The department examination coordinator allocates setting and moderation. Faculty upload moderated papers through the secure portal and submit verified marks to the department coordinator within four calendar days so the department can meet the five-day institutional deadline. Examination content must not be sent to a public AI service.

## 6. Department data stewardship

Each Head of Department designates a faculty data steward. Faculty store grades, attendance, accommodations, and advising notes only in approved systems; complete quarterly access reviews when asked; report a suspected disclosure within 24 hours; and cooperate with scheduled disposal.

## 7. Student support

Faculty publish consultation time, make reasonable referrals, and document only information needed for academic support. Disability details are restricted to personnel implementing the approved adjustment.
