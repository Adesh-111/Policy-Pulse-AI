# Northbridge College Examination Administration Policy

**Version:** 3.2 (Old)  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2024  
**Policy owner:** Examination Cell

## 1. Examination schedule

The Examination Cell publishes the final timetable at least 21 calendar days before the first examination. A department may request a correction within three working days of publication. Later changes require the Controller of Examinations' written approval.

## 2. Question papers

The Head of Department nominates two paper setters for each course. A sealed final paper and marking scheme must reach the Examination Cell **ten business days before the examination**. The Examination Cell completes formatting, secure printing, and custody. Generative software must not receive unpublished question papers.

## 3. Candidate eligibility

The Examination Cell accepts the eligibility list certified by each Head of Department under the attendance policy. A corrected list may be submitted until 48 hours before the examination when the Head supplies reasons.

## 4. Reasonable accommodation

Students seeking extra time or a scribe submit supporting documents to Student Affairs at least seven working days before the examination. Student Affairs sends approved arrangements to the Examination Cell.

## 5. Mark submission

Each examiner submits verified marks to the Examination Cell within **ten calendar days after the examination**. The Examination Cell performs range and absence checks. Only the Controller of Examinations may reopen a locked mark sheet.

## 6. Malpractice review

An invigilator reports suspected malpractice before leaving the venue. The Examination Discipline Committee gives the student notice and an opportunity to respond. Evidence is retained with the case file for five years.

## 7. Results and corrections

The Examination Cell publishes results after the moderation board approves them. Clerical correction requests must be made within 30 calendar days. Answer scripts and mark sheets are retained for **five years** and then securely destroyed unless litigation or an appeal requires a hold.
