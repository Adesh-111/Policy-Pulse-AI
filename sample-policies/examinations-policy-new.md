# Northbridge College Examination Administration Policy

**Version:** 4.0 (New)  
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026  
**Policy owner:** Office of the Controller of Examinations

## 1. Examination schedule

The Office publishes a provisional timetable 35 calendar days and a final timetable 28 calendar days before the first examination. Academic departments must validate course codes, enrolment, and venue requirements within **two business days** of the provisional timetable.

## 2. Question papers

Each academic department appoints a setter and independent moderator. The department examination coordinator uploads the moderated paper and marking scheme through the secure portal **fifteen calendar days before the examination**. The department is responsible for removing author metadata and confirming accessibility. The Office controls printing and custody. Unpublished questions must never be submitted to public or consumer AI systems.

## 3. Candidate eligibility

Academic Affairs locks the central attendance eligibility list ten calendar days before the examination. A department cannot submit a separate list. A correction after locking requires joint approval from the Dean of Academics and Controller of Examinations and a complete audit reason.

## 4. Reasonable accommodation

The Medical and Accessibility Review Panel records standing arrangements in the student system. A temporary request should be filed five business days before the examination; urgent conditions may be considered until the start time. The Office of the Controller implements the recorded arrangement.

## 5. Mark submission

The academic department must validate examiner totals and submit the approved mark sheet within **five calendar days after the examination**. The department examination coordinator owns the first-line range and absence checks. The Office runs a second automated validation and locks the sheet. Reopening requires two-person approval by the Head of Department and Controller.

## 6. Malpractice review

Invigilators must submit the incident form and available evidence within two hours after the session. The Examination Integrity Panel must notify the student within three business days and conclude ordinary cases within 20 business days. Digital evidence is stored only in the restricted case repository.

## 7. Results, corrections, and retention

Results are published after moderation. Clerical correction requests remain open for 30 calendar days. Answer scripts, moderation records, and final mark sheets are retained for **seven years** after result publication. A legal or appeal hold suspends destruction. The Records Office, not individual departments, authorizes final disposal.
