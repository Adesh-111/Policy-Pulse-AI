# Northbridge College Attendance and Academic Participation Policy

**Version:** 2.0 (New)  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026  
**Policy owner:** Academic Affairs Division  
**Applies to:** All undergraduate students and teaching departments

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy establishes participation requirements for lectures, tutorials, studios, laboratories, clinical sessions, and approved online synchronous classes. Attendance is assessed course by course. Approved fieldwork counts as attendance only after the supervising department uploads a verified roster.

## 2. Minimum attendance

Students must attend at least **80% of the scheduled meetings in each registered course**. Laboratory and clinical courses require **85% attendance** unless a statutory council mandates a higher level. Thresholds cannot be reduced in a course handbook or by a department resolution.

A student below the applicable threshold is ineligible for the end-semester examination unless an exemption under section 3 or duty leave under section 4 brings the student within the permitted shortage.

## 3. Medical and disability-related exemption

Medical condonation may cover no more than **five percentage points** below the applicable threshold. A student must upload a legible certificate from a registered medical practitioner within **three business days after returning to class**. A newly established Medical and Accessibility Review Panel, rather than a Head of Department acting alone, decides the request.

Ongoing disability-related attendance adjustments are considered individually and are not subject to the five-point medical cap when recorded in an approved accessibility plan. A request first submitted more than 30 calendar days after the absence is not considered.

## 4. Co-curricular duty

Pre-approved representation of the college is treated as duty leave. The sponsoring office must enter the roster in the attendance system before the event. Emergency substitutions may be entered within two business days after the event with the Dean of Student Experience's approval.

## 5. Monitoring and notice

Course teachers must verify attendance weekly. The central system sends an automated warning when a student's course attendance first falls below 85%. Academic advisors must contact the student within five business days and record the intervention. Departments publish a final eligibility list at least ten calendar days before the examination.

## 6. Responsibility

Course teachers verify source records; academic advisors document early intervention; Academic Affairs operates the monitoring system; and the Medical and Accessibility Review Panel decides exemptions. Heads of Department certify data completeness but no longer decide medical condonation. The Examination Cell consumes the centrally locked eligibility list.

## 7. Appeals

A student may appeal either a recording error or the application of an approved accessibility plan within five business days of the final list. The Academic Appeals Committee issues a reasoned decision before the examination. It cannot waive the ordinary 80% minimum outside sections 3 and 4.
