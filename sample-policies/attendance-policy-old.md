# Northbridge College Attendance and Academic Participation Policy

**Version:** 1.4 (Old)  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2024  
**Policy owner:** Student Affairs Office  
**Applies to:** All undergraduate students and teaching departments

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy establishes the minimum participation required for a student to remain eligible for end-semester assessment. Attendance is recorded for every scheduled lecture, tutorial, studio, and laboratory. Approved fieldwork is treated as attended when the supervising department submits the roster.

## 2. Minimum attendance

Students must attend at least **75% of the scheduled meetings in each registered course**. A course, not a semester-wide average, is the unit used to determine eligibility. Departments may prescribe an 80% minimum for a laboratory or clinical course when that higher threshold appears in the approved course handbook before registration.

A student below the applicable threshold is ordinarily ineligible to sit the end-semester examination for that course.

## 3. Medical condonation

The Head of Department may condone a shortage of no more than **10 percentage points** when the absence is caused by documented illness or hospitalization. The student must submit the original medical certificate to the department office within **seven calendar days after returning to class**. Certificates submitted after the semester ends are not considered unless the Principal records exceptional circumstances.

Condonation does not convert an absence into attendance. The approved shortage is considered only when examination eligibility is decided.

## 4. Co-curricular duty

Absence while officially representing the college in sport, cultural activity, the National Service Scheme, or an approved competition is treated as duty leave. Student Affairs must approve the event in advance and send the participant list to the relevant department within five working days after the event.

## 5. Monitoring and notice

Each academic department must close its attendance register on the final working day of every month. The department posts a shortage list on its notice board within five working days. Students have seven calendar days to report a recording error to the course teacher.

## 6. Responsibility

Course teachers maintain source attendance records. Heads of Department decide medical condonation and certify the final eligibility list. Student Affairs maintains duty-leave approvals. The Examination Cell relies on the eligibility list certified by each Head of Department.

## 7. Appeals

A student may appeal a clerical error to the Dean of Academics within five working days of the final list. The appeal is limited to incorrect recording or calculation; the Dean cannot waive the 75% requirement.
