# Northbridge College Privacy, Records, and Data Stewardship Policy

**Version:** 3.0 (New)  
**Effective date:** 1 April 2026  
**Policy owner:** Data Protection Office

## 1. Principles and scope

Personal data must be collected for a documented purpose, minimized, accurate, access-controlled, and retained under an approved schedule. The policy covers college systems, approved cloud services, paper records, analytics datasets, and data sent to an AI service.

## 2. Distributed responsibility

The Data Protection Officer owns the privacy program. Each department head must appoint a **department data steward** who maintains a processing inventory, reviews access quarterly, and coordinates deletion. Information Technology implements security controls but is no longer the sole privacy owner. The Registrar remains custodian of official student records.

## 3. Approved processing and vendors

Sensitive data may be processed only in an approved system with least-privilege access. A privacy and security review is required before a new vendor or generative AI service receives college data. Public links, personal storage accounts, and production data copied to unapproved test systems are prohibited.

## 4. Retention schedule

Routine student-service records, advising notes, attendance source logs, and unsuccessful applications are retained for **three years after the last active relationship**. Final transcripts and degree registers are permanent. Examination answer scripts, moderation records, and final mark sheets are retained for **seven years after results are published**. Staff employment records are retained for six years after employment ends.

The department data steward initiates disposal; the Records Office validates the schedule; and Information Technology executes verified deletion. Legal, investigation, and appeal holds suspend disposal.

## 5. Incident response

Any suspected loss, misdirection, unauthorized access, or disclosure must be reported to the service desk and Data Protection Office **within 24 hours of discovery**. The incident lead records containment, affected data, and notification decisions. Personnel must preserve evidence and must not investigate by accessing another user's account.

## 6. Individual rights requests

The Data Protection Office coordinates access and correction requests. A department data steward must return search results within seven business days and explain any system that was not searched.
